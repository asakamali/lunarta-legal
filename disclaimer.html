Regulatory and recall information

Recall and regulatory information appears in Lunarta's dedicated Recalls section and is separate from safety-search results. A safety-search result or classification does not indicate that Lunarta has checked the product for recalls or other regulatory notices.

Lunarta's Recalls section displays information from official sources such as the FDA, USDA, and CPSC. Items may be displayed as recalls, safety alerts, investigations, market withdrawals, public health alerts, or other current notices based on information published by those agencies.

Some notices are published before a formal recall is classified or may never become a formal recall. When regulatory classification is unavailable or cannot reasonably be determined from the available information, Lunarta may label the item as a Current Notice.

FDA: Lunarta uses multiple FDA publications, which may include the FDA Enforcement Report database, news releases, safety communications, and RSS or other data feeds. Not every FDA publication represents a formal recall. Some may concern safety alerts, investigations, market withdrawals, or other consumer communications.

Regulatory agencies may update, correct, reclassify, supplement, or remove information over time, and Lunarta's display of that information may not always reflect those changes immediately.

The absence of a recall or regulatory notice does not mean that a product is safe, approved, recommended, or free from defects or health risks. A "no recall found" or similar result means only that Lunarta did not identify a matching notice within the sources and search parameters used at that time.

Recall results may also depend on product names, manufacturers, lot or batch numbers, model numbers, dates, geographic areas, and other identifying information. Lunarta cannot guarantee that every applicable recall, safety notice, or affected product will be identified.

For important safety decisions, review the original agency publication and, when appropriate, consult a qualified healthcare professional.
