This Privacy Policy explains what information the Lunarta Mama app ("Lunarta", "we", "us") collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. Lunarta is a tool that provides informational safety guidance about foods, medications, and products during pregnancy and breastfeeding.

No account required

Lunarta does not require you to create an account or sign in. There is no username, password, or personal profile stored on our servers.

Information stored only on your device

The following information is stored locally on your device using standard app storage and is not uploaded to a Lunarta-controlled database:

  • Your selected mode (pregnancy or breastfeeding)
  • Items you've saved for later reference
  • Your daily search count (used only to apply the free-tier limit)
  • Your subscription status

Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage permanently deletes this locally stored data. We have no way to recover it because we do not maintain a server-side copy.

What we send to make the app work

When you search for an item or scan a barcode, the text of your query (and, for barcode scans, the barcode number) is sent to our backend server so it can:

  • Ask Anthropic's Claude API to generate a safety summary
  • Look up product information from Open Food Facts for barcode scans

Lunarta does not intentionally attach your name, email address, or personal profile to safety-search or barcode requests. Only the search text or barcode needed to perform the requested function is sent as part of the request.

Our server may temporarily process technical information such as an IP address to operate security, abuse-prevention, and usage-limit features. This information is not used to create a profile about you.

All communication between the app and our servers uses encrypted HTTPS connections. When information is sent to third-party services such as Anthropic or Open Food Facts to provide a requested feature, that information is also subject to those services' applicable privacy practices.

Health-related searches

Lunarta is designed to minimize the collection and processing of personal and health-related information. We do not ask you to provide your name, medical records, diagnoses, health history, or other identifying health information in order to use the app.

Because Lunarta provides pregnancy and breastfeeding safety guidance, information you choose to enter in a search may itself contain health-related information. Search text is processed only as needed to provide the safety information you request and operate the service.

Lunarta does not use your search text to create a health profile about you, for advertising, or to sell information about you.

Please do not include your name or other unnecessary identifying or medical information in search queries.

Recalls and safety notices

The Recalls section separately displays recent recalls and safety notices retrieved from public sources maintained by the U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission (CPSC), U.S. Department of Agriculture (USDA), and U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA).

Recall results may be filtered by state within the app. Your selected state is used only on your device to filter already retrieved recall information and is not sent to our servers for recall filtering.

Recall and safety-notice information comes from public U.S. government sources.

Help and feedback

If you submit feedback through the Help & Feedback feature, the feedback text you provide, along with basic technical information such as your device platform and app version, is sent through our transactional email provider, Brevo, so we can receive your message and troubleshoot issues.

Providing an email address is optional. If you select "I'd like a reply" and provide an email address, it is used to respond to your message. We do not add that email address to a marketing list or use it for advertising.

Information processed by our service providers may be retained by those providers according to their own applicable privacy policies and data-retention practices.

Subscriptions and payment

Lunarta Premium is sold as an auto-renewing subscription through the Apple App Store or Google Play. Payment, billing, and subscription management are handled by Apple or Google. Lunarta does not receive or store your payment card details.

Lunarta receives information necessary to determine whether your subscription is active so the app can provide Premium features.

No advertising, sale of information, or behavioral tracking

Lunarta does not show third-party advertising, does not use third-party advertising or behavioral-tracking SDKs, and does not sell your personal or health-related information to data brokers or advertisers.

Lunarta does not use your searches for targeted or behavioral advertising.

Children's privacy

Lunarta is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Your privacy choices

Because Lunarta does not require an account and information such as saved items, selected mode, and search-count information is stored locally on your device, you can delete that locally stored information by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

You may choose not to provide an email address when submitting feedback.

You may also choose not to include personal or identifying information in your searches.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes — for example, if we add optional accounts, additional data collection, analytics, or other features in the future — we'll update this page and adjust the "Last updated" date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy or Lunarta's privacy practices? Use the Help & Feedback option in the Lunarta app (tap the menu icon on the Search screen), or email us at support@lunarta.io.
