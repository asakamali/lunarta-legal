This Washington Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy describes how Lunarta Mama ("Lunarta," "we," "us") handles consumer health data that may be subject to the Washington My Health My Data Act ("MHMDA").

This policy is separate from Lunarta's general Privacy Policy and applies to consumer health data covered by Washington law.

Consumer health data we may collect

Lunarta provides informational safety guidance about foods, medications, products, and other topics during pregnancy and breastfeeding.

We do not require you to provide medical records, diagnoses, health history, or other identifying medical information to use Lunarta.

However, because Lunarta provides pregnancy and breastfeeding safety guidance, information you voluntarily enter into a search may itself contain or reveal health-related information.

Depending on what you choose to enter, the categories of consumer health data Lunarta may process include:

  • Pregnancy- or breastfeeding-related information contained in a search
  • Information about medications, supplements, foods, ingredients, products, or exposures that you enter in a health-related search
  • Other health-related information that you voluntarily include in search text

We ask that you not include your name or other unnecessary identifying or medical information in search queries.

Sources of consumer health data

Consumer health data processed through Lunarta's safety-search feature comes directly from information you choose to enter.

Lunarta does not obtain your medical records from healthcare providers, insurers, pharmacies, data brokers, or similar sources.

Why we collect and use consumer health data

Lunarta processes health-related search text as necessary to provide the information and functionality you request.

For example, if you search for information about whether a medication, food, ingredient, or product is appropriate during pregnancy or breastfeeding, Lunarta processes the search text to generate the requested informational safety summary and operate the service.

We do not use consumer health data to:

  • Create advertising or marketing profiles about you
  • Provide targeted or behavioral advertising
  • Sell consumer health data

Consumer health data we share

To the extent your search text contains consumer health data, Lunarta may share the search text necessary to generate your requested safety summary with service providers that process the information on our behalf.

The categories of consumer health data that may be shared are therefore the health-related information that you voluntarily include in safety-search text.

Lunarta does not sell consumer health data.

Third parties and affiliates

Lunarta uses Anthropic to provide the AI service that generates safety summaries. Search text needed to provide the safety summary you request may therefore be transmitted to and processed by Anthropic.

Lunarta does not intentionally attach your name, email address, or a Lunarta user profile to safety-search text sent to Anthropic.

Open Food Facts may process barcode or product-identification information when you use barcode features. Lunarta does not intentionally send health-related search information to Open Food Facts as part of a barcode product lookup.

Brevo provides transactional email services for Lunarta's Help & Feedback feature. Please do not include health or medical information in feedback messages. If you voluntarily include health-related information in a feedback message, that information may be processed by Brevo in order to transmit your message.

Lunarta does not currently have affiliates with which it shares consumer health data.

Recalls and state filtering

Lunarta's Recalls section operates separately from safety searches and displays public regulatory information.

If you select a state to filter recall information, the selected state is used locally on your device to filter already retrieved recall records. Your selected state is not sent to Lunarta's servers for recall filtering.

Lunarta does not use your recall-state selection to create a health profile about you.

Your Washington consumer health data rights

Subject to Washington law, you may have the right to:

  • Confirm whether Lunarta is collecting, sharing, or selling consumer health data concerning you
  • Access consumer health data concerning you
  • Obtain information about the third parties and affiliates with whom your consumer health data has been shared or sold
  • Withdraw consent from future collection or sharing where consent is required
  • Request deletion of consumer health data concerning you

Lunarta does not sell consumer health data.

Lunarta will not unlawfully discriminate against you for exercising rights provided by the Washington My Health My Data Act.

How to exercise your rights

To submit a request concerning your Washington consumer health data rights, email: support@lunarta.io

Please include "Washington Consumer Health Data Request" in the subject line.

Because Lunarta does not require an account and does not intentionally associate safety-search text with your name, email address, or a persistent Lunarta user profile, we may have limited ability to identify particular search data as belonging to you.

We may request information reasonably necessary to authenticate your request. We will not require you to create an account in order to exercise your rights.

We will respond to authenticated requests as required by applicable law.

Deletion requests

If we can identify consumer health data concerning you that is subject to a valid deletion request, we will delete that information as required by applicable law and notify processors, contractors, affiliates, and other third parties as required by Washington law.

Deletion from archived or backup systems may take additional time where permitted by applicable law.

Withdrawal of consent

Where Lunarta relies on your consent to collect or share consumer health data, you may withdraw that consent for future collection or sharing by contacting: support@lunarta.io

Withdrawal of consent does not affect processing that is otherwise permitted by law, including processing necessary to provide a product or service you request.

Data security

Lunarta uses reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect consumer health data.

Communications between the Lunarta app and our servers use encrypted HTTPS connections.

Access to consumer health data is limited as appropriate to personnel, processors, and service providers for which access is necessary to provide the requested service or operate and protect Lunarta.

Sale of consumer health data

Lunarta does not sell consumer health data.

Lunarta does not use consumer health data for targeted or behavioral advertising.

Changes to our consumer health data practices

Lunarta will not collect, use, or share additional categories of consumer health data, or use consumer health data for additional purposes, without providing the disclosures and obtaining consent required by applicable Washington law.

If our consumer health data practices change, we will update this policy and the "Last updated" date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy or Lunarta's consumer health data practices may be sent to: support@lunarta.io
